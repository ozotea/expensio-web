Questions

Good to know

How does auto-detecting transactions work?

On Android, with your permission, Expensio reads incoming bank and UPI notifications on your device, extracts the amount, merchant and account, and logs the transaction. Recognised accounts are captured automatically; anything ambiguous waits as a one-tap confirmation. The parsing happens entirely on your phone — nothing is ever uploaded.

On iPhone this isn’t possible — Apple doesn’t let any app read notifications or SMS. Instead you add a spend by voice in the app or by asking Siri, which takes about as long as reading the notification would.

Which platforms is Expensio on?

Expensio is on Android today, and lands on iPhone in September 2026. Everything core is identical on both — accounts, credit-card cycles, EMIs, budgets, insights, backups and offline-first privacy.

Two things differ at iOS launch. Automatic capture from bank notifications is Android-only, because Apple doesn’t permit it — iPhone users add a spend by voice or via Siri. Home-screen widgets and app-icon shortcuts are also Android-only for now, and follow on iOS in a later release.

Is my financial data private and secure?

Yes. Expensio is offline-first with no ads and no third-party trackers. Your data lives in a local database on your device, protected by an optional biometric App Lock. Backups go only to your own Google Drive, and full card numbers and CVVs are never stored.

Does it work without internet?

Completely. Every core feature — tracking, budgets, analytics, cards and EMIs — works fully offline. An internet connection is only needed for optional extras like Google Drive backup and live currency rates.

Is Expensio free?

Yes — the core experience is free, including expense & income tracking, budgets and App Lock. Expensio Pro unlocks unlimited accounts & cards, advanced statistics, PDF statements, custom categories and Expensio AI, with a free trial.

Can I back up and move my data?

Absolutely. Auto and manual backups sync to your own Google Drive, and you can export your full history to CSV or JSON at any time. You own your data, always.
