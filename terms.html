2. Description of Service

Expensio is a personal expense and money-tracking app offering transactions, budgets, accounts, credit cards, analytics, brand tagging, savings goals, statements and PDF export, Google Drive backup, home-screen widgets and voice entry. It is available on Android today; the iPhone app is released in September 2026.

Some features are specific to one platform. Auto-capture from bank notifications is Android only, and permanently so — Apple provides no way for an app to read another app’s notifications or your SMS. Home-screen widgets and app-icon shortcuts are also Android only at present and are planned for a later iPhone release. On iPhone, quick entry is instead handled by Siri and Shortcuts, which also surface the app through Spotlight, the Action button and Control Center. Voice Quick Add, card scanning and every other feature work the same on both platforms.

Expensio is not a bank, payment processor or financial advisor. The core tracking and budgeting features are free; an optional Pro subscription unlocks advanced features.
